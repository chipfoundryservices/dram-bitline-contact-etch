# Appendix G — Further Reading

The following are standard starting points. Citation details should be checked against the originals.

- M. A. Lieberman and A. J. Lichtenberg, *Principles of Plasma Discharges and Materials Processing*, 2nd ed., Wiley, 2005.
- G. S. Oehrlein and Y. Kurogi, "Sidewall surface chemistry in directional etching processes," *Materials Science and Engineering R* 24, 153 (1998).
- R. A. Gottscho, C. W. Jurgensen and D. J. Vitkavage, "Microscopic uniformity in plasma etching," *J. Vac. Sci. Technol. B* 10, 2133 (1992).
- J. W. Coburn and H. F. Winters, "Conductance considerations in the reactive ion etching of high aspect ratio features," *Appl. Phys. Lett.* 55, 2730 (1989).
- M. Schaepkens, T. E. F. M. Standaert, N. R. Rueger, P. G. M. Sebel, G. S. Oehrlein and J. M. Cook, "Study of the SiO₂-to-Si₃N₄ etch selectivity mechanism in inductively coupled fluorocarbon plasmas," *J. Vac. Sci. Technol. A* 17, 26 (1999).
- K. J. Kanarik et al., "Overview of atomic layer etching in the semiconductor industry," *J. Vac. Sci. Technol. A* 33, 020802 (2015).
- B. Keeth, R. J. Baker, B. Johnson and F. Lin, *DRAM Circuit Design: Fundamental and High-Speed Topics*, Wiley-IEEE, 2008.
- D. K. Schroder, *Semiconductor Material and Device Characterization*, 3rd ed., Wiley, 2006 (contact resistance and transmission-line methods).
- H. Ogawa et al. and others, papers on NF₃/NH₃ remote-plasma pre-cleans for contact formation, *J. Vac. Sci. Technol.* and *Jpn. J. Appl. Phys.*, 2000s.
- Papers on buried-channel array transistors, bit-line contacts and 4F² vertical-channel DRAM, *VLSI Symposium* and *IEDM* proceedings, 2010s–2020s.
- J. R. Howell, *A Catalog of Radiation Heat Transfer Configuration Factors* (disk-to-disk and other view factors).
