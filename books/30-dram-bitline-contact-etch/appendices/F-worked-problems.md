# Appendix F — Worked Problems

**F.1** A DC is 31 nm at the buffer top. The 20 nm buffer is etched at 82°. What is the bottom CD, and is the contact node-limited?
*Answer:* 2 × 20 × cot 82° = 5.6 nm, so *D*_b = 25.4 nm. Between the 22 and 26 nm rows of Chapter 10 the area at zero overlay is 260–272 nm², 95–99 % of the node. The contact is nearly node-limited.

**F.2** With recipe A, a lot's mean bottom CD is 24.5 nm instead of 26 nm. How should the recess time change?
*Answer:* the recess at fixed time drops by 0.55 × 1.5 ≈ 0.8 nm. At 0.49 nm/s, add about 1.7 s.

**F.3** The process uses recipe B to 25 nm of silicon recess. The incoming cap is 2 nm below the Si surface, the buffer overetch removes 3 nm, and the clean removes 2 nm of liner oxide. How much cap is left over the word-line metal under the liner pit?
*Answer:* 60 − 2 − 3 − 27.6 − 2 ≈ 25 nm, below a 30 nm minimum. Shorten the B step, or switch to A for the last 15 nm.

**F.4** A DC at 26 nm bottom CD has 6 nm of overlay along the word line. Estimate *R*_DC and the clearance to the neighbour.
*Answer:* from Appendix B.6, area 235 nm², so *R* = 1.5 × 10⁶/235 + 847 ≈ 7.2 kΩ. The clearance is 5.4 nm.

**F.5** Overlay is 1.8 nm (1σ per axis) and resistance fails above 10 nm of radial error. Estimate the failure rate.
*Answer:* exp(−100/(2 × 3.24)) = exp(−15.4) ≈ 2 × 10⁻⁷, about 0.2 ppm.

**F.6** A mesa 4 nm tall is left by a short dielectric-first step at *D* = 26 nm. If the sidewall counts at half the efficiency of the top, what is the effective area and *R*_DC?
*Answer:* 272 + 0.5 × 63 × 4 = 398 nm²; *R* = 1.5 × 10⁶/398 + 847 ≈ 4.6 kΩ.
